# Cold email templates

Fill every placeholder from the CV or the sheet. If a value is missing, ask; never invent it. Keep under 150 words.

## Variant A: application to a posted role

Subject: Application: {role} | {name} | {years} yrs {core_skill}

Hi {contact_or_team},

I'm applying for the {role} position I saw on {source} ({post_date}).

{one_line_match: a real project or result from the CV that matches the posting}

GitHub: {github}
Portfolio: {portfolio}
LinkedIn: {linkedin}

I'm based in {city}, available {notice_or_joining}, and my resume is attached.

Thanks,
{name}
{phone} | {email}

## Variant B: interest in a company (no posted role)

Subject: Interest in {role} at {company}

Hi {contact_or_team},

I came across {company} on {source} and I'm interested in {role} roles. {one_line_match}

GitHub: {github}
Portfolio: {portfolio}
LinkedIn: {linkedin}

I'm based in {city}, available {notice_or_joining}, and my resume is attached.

Thanks,
{name}
{phone} | {email}

## Variant C: student or fresher

Same as A, with one added line before the logistics:

I graduate in {graduation_year} from {college} and am looking for {role_type} roles.

## Checklist before saving the draft

- Address came from the sheet with a recorded source
- No claim not on the CV
- Years calculated per references/profile-extraction.md
- Resume attached or the user told to attach it
